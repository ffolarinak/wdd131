Taste of Nigeria

Website Planning Document

1. Site Name

Taste of Nigeria

The name represents a website dedicated to introducing Nigerian traditional foods, flavours, and food culture. I chose this name because it is simple, memorable, and communicates the main purpose of the website.

Proposed domain: tasteofnigeria.org

Logo idea: A cooking pot representing Nigerian food and cultural heritage.

2. Site Purpose

Taste of Nigeria will provide information about popular Nigerian traditional dishes, their ingredients, preparation methods, and cultural importance. Visitors will learn about foods from different parts of Nigeria and discover the variety of flavours in Nigerian cuisine.

3. Scenarios

These are questions visitors may ask when using the website:

  • What ingredients are needed to prepare Nigerian Egusi soup?
  • What are some popular traditional foods in Nigeria?
  • How is Jollof rice prepared?
  • Which Nigerian foods are commonly eaten during celebrations?

4. Color Schema

Deep Green (#176B45): Used for the header, footer, headings, and navigation.

Warm Cream (#FFF8EE): Used as the main page background.

Burnt Orange (#D97706): Used for accents, highlights, and section borders.

5. Typography

Headings: Poppins

Poppins will be used for the main title and section headings because it is clear and modern.

Body Text: Open Sans

Open Sans will be used for paragraphs, descriptions, and general website content because it is easy to read.
